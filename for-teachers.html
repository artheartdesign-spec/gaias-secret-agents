Print. Brief. Investigate.

01 / PREPARE

Choose one nearby place

A tree, garden or potted plant is enough. Check the area and arrange access and supervision using your school procedures.

02 / EXPLORE

Look for evidence

Working in pairs, children draw one living thing and identify something in its surroundings that helps meet a need.

03 / REPORT

Explain and care

Agents share what they noticed, explain their thinking and choose a simple way to respect the place they investigated.

Designed for a manageable print run

  • Print page 2 once per pair: 15 sheets for 30 children.
  • Keep the three teacher pages on hand.
  • Use pencils and firm writing surfaces.
  • No cutting, collecting or special equipment.
  • Children can draw, point or dictate their observations.

The student page has a white background for economical printing. This pilot is designed around 45 minutes; teacher trials will check the timing.

A clear learning purpose

Objective: identify how one place helps meet the needs of a plant or animal.

Suggested Year 1 Science connections: AC9S1U01, with selected observation and communication elements of AC9S1I03 and AC9S1I06.

The pack explains the evidence for each connection and the limits of coverage. Teachers should check their local planning requirements. No ACARA endorsement is claimed.

Before your agents begin

What if there are no flowers or animals to see?

Choose a tree, grass or a potted plant. The investigation does not depend on flowers or animal sightings.

Can we run it indoors?

Yes. Use a teacher-provided potted plant near a window, or a clear teacher-selected photo of a familiar school place. The teacher notes explain how to distinguish observations from guesses.

Can I use the free pack again?

Yes. You may print and reuse it with your own and future classes and send completed sheets home. Share the official download link with colleagues. The pack contains the full copying permission.

Is the paid Starter Kit licensed for a whole class?

This free pilot has its own classroom permission. Please do not assume family products include classroom copying rights. A separate classroom edition is being prepared; school enquiries can go to Headquarters.
