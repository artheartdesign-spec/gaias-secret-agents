Refund Policy

Clear support and refund information for Gaia’s Secret Agents printable digital products.

Digital downloads

Because digital files can be accessed immediately, we generally do not offer a change-of-mind refund after a file has been successfully delivered. We will still assess reasonable requests fairly, and this does not limit any rights you have under law.

If there is a problem

Contact us if:

  • the download link does not work or the file cannot be accessed;
  • pages are missing, corrupted or materially different from the product description;
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase; or
  • the product otherwise fails to meet an applicable consumer guarantee.

Depending on the problem and applicable law, we may restore access, provide a corrected or replacement file, or issue a full or partial refund.

Australian Consumer Law

Digital products supplied to Australian consumers come with consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded. If a product has a major problem, you may be entitled to choose a refund or replacement. Other remedies may apply to a minor problem. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

How to request help

Email hello@gaiassecretagents.com with the purchase email address, product name and Gumroad receipt or order number if available. Do not send payment-card details or passwords.

Approved refunds are processed through the payment platform used for the purchase. Processing time may depend on that platform and your financial institution.

Download missing?

We can usually restore access without requiring another purchase. Email us and tell us which file you expected to receive.
