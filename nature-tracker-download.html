5-Minute Nature Mission

Build a Mini Wildlife Habitat

Then use your Nature Tracker to discover who visits.

MISSION GOALCreate a tiny safe shelter using natural materials you can find nearby.
What you need
  • A quiet shady spot outdoors
  • Dry leaves, bark or small sticks
  • A few stones or pine cones
  • Optional: short hollow stems or cardboard tubes
Your mission
1
FIND
Collect a few fallen natural materials. Leave living plants where they are.
2
BUILD
Make a small sheltered nook. Keep it low, stable and open enough for tiny visitors.
3
PLACE
Put it somewhere quiet, away from paths and pets.
4
OBSERVE
Check once a day. Look, don’t disturb. Record clues on your tracker.
AGENT RULE: Observe wildlife without touching, trapping or feeding it. If an animal moves in, give it space.
