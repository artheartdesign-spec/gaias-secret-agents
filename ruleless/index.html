Questions

Before you buy.

Is RULELESS only for kids?

No. RULELESS is suitable for all ages. It is designed so children, teenagers, parents, grandparents and friends can play together.

Is this a physical card deck?

No. This version is a digital printable download. You print and cut the cards yourself.

Do I have to follow every card?

No. That is the point. Pass, play or pick another. There is no score and no penalty.

What do I need?

A printer, A4 paper or card, scissors and a few minutes to cut the deck. Adult supervision is recommended when children use scissors.
