Adventure makes the memories.

Gaia’s Secret Agents helps families step away from screens, explore the natural world and enjoy meaningful time together. Follow your curiosity, notice the small wonders, and learn from one another along the way.

Find your family’s first adventureWhy explore together?
More than an activity

Everyone has something to discover — and something to share.

A child might spot a tiny insect an adult has walked past. A grandparent might remember the name of a bird. Someone else might ask the question that sends the whole family exploring. There is no single expert here: curiosity belongs to everyone.

Our nature missions make space for conversation, cooperation, laughter and the kind of shared moments that are hard to create when everyone is looking at a screen.

01 / NOTICE

Slow down and look closer

Find patterns in leaves, listen for birds, follow clouds or discover what lives in a patch of garden. Small wonders are everywhere.

02 / LEARN TOGETHER

Share what each of you knows

Ask questions, swap stories, make guesses and learn from one another. Every age brings a different way of seeing the world.

03 / CARE

Feel part of the natural world

Enjoy being outside and take small, thoughtful actions that help protect the places and living things you meet.

Made for real life

Go at your own pace.

Head to a local park, beach, walking track, backyard or balcony. You do not need special equipment, expert knowledge or a perfect weekend. Start with what is around you.

“The best discoveries are the ones we get to share.”
  • Screen-free time outdoors

Your next shared memory

Start small. Step outside together.

Choose a nearby natural place. Each person names one thing they notice, one thing they wonder about and one thing they would like to learn. Then follow the most interesting question together.

That is a family nature mission: simple, screen-free, and open to everyone.
