How to play

Print the A4 sheets, cut out the cards, shuffle the deck and take turns drawing one. Read it aloud, then talk, imagine or complete the challenge together. Anyone can play or pass. There are no winners or losers.

Designed for: family game nights, holidays, camping, grandparents and grandchildren, and everyday screen-free fun.
