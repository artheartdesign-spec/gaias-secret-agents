**Campaign idea:** Turn “let’s get off screens” into “let’s discover something together.”

## Message hierarchy

1. **Lead with the shared experience:** a child discovers something outside, then brings the evidence to a grown-up.
2. **Explain the product:** a story-led, screen-free printable adventure, not a generic worksheet pack.
3. **Make the next step clear:** visit the Starter Kit page.
4. **Keep the promise grounded:** no claims of guaranteed learning outcomes, behaviour change or scientific validation.
