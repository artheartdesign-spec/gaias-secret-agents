## Email 2 — Make the first mission easy

**Subject:** A small field mission for your next outing  
**Preview text:** You do not need special equipment or expert nature knowledge.

Hello, Agent’s Grown-Up,

A useful nature adventure can begin with one ordinary question:

**What have we never stopped to look at properly?**

On your next walk, in the garden, or from a window, invite your Agent to choose one thing to observe. It could be a leaf, a bird, a patch of soil, a cloud, or an insect viewed without disturbing it.

Try asking:
- What do you notice first?
- What makes you curious?
- What would you like to find out?
- Is there one small way we could care for this place?

Let the child’s observations lead. You do not need to turn it into a lesson, and there is no single “correct” discovery.
