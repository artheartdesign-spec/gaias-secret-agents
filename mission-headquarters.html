Questions

Mission Headquarters FAQ

What age are GSA missions for?

The core GSA experience is designed for children aged 6–11, with grown-up preparation where needed.

Are the missions screen-free?

Yes. The activities are designed around paper, observation, conversation, imagination and real-world exploration rather than a device.

Is Ocean Discoverer available to buy yet?

Not yet. The release page is collecting grown-up emails while the full paid mission pack is completed. The Starter Kit is currently the main paid digital product.

Do children need an email address?

No. Grown-ups handle email sign-up and purchasing.
