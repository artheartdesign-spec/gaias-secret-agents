The physical collection is in preparation. This page shows the new artwork. Product details, prices, delivery information and checkout links will be added when the collection is ready to order.

Are these physical products or printable downloads?

The upcoming sticker and tote collection is planned as physical merchandise. Our Starter Kit and Nature Habitat art prints are available separately as digital downloads.

Who are the Gaia’s Secret Agents characters?

Bramblewicks is our fox Keeper of the Files. Kai the green sea turtle is the Wise Guide of the Ocean, Flip the dolphin is the Ocean Scout, Slate the wolf is the Land Guardian, Astra is our owl and Sprig is our tree sprite.
