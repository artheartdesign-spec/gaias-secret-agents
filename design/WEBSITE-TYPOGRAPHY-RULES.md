# Gaia’s Secret Agents — Website Typography Rules

Status: APPROVED / LOCKED — 21 November 2026

## Mandatory
- Do not use tiny uppercase or all-caps typography for headings, labels, navigation, buttons, cards, or body copy.
- Avoid excessive letter spacing and generic corporate typography.
- Use readable mixed-case text throughout the website.
- Headlines: large, beautiful, magical storybook-inspired serif type, with clear hierarchy.
- Body: clean, elegant, highly legible mixed-case text.
- Buttons and navigation: prominent, inviting, comfortably sized and readable on iPhone.
- Prefer legibility and contrast over decorative lettering; avoid text-heavy sections.
- Overall aesthetic: premium illustrated adventure storybook, mystical and immersive, not a corporate brochure.

## Preservation
- Approved logo, art, copy, taglines and layouts remain locked. Do not redraw or replace them without explicit approval.
- This file governs future website work; it does not authorize unrelated redesigns.
