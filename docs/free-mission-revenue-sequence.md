# Gaia’s Secret Agents — Free Mission Revenue Sequence

Purpose: convert an adult who requests the free mission into (1) mission completion, (2) Starter Kit purchase, and then (3) interest in ongoing monthly missions.

## Funnel

Free mission signup → Mission download → Report Back → Field Verified + Impact Record → Starter Kit A$19 → Mission 001 / monthly mission interest

Core positioning:

**Small Agents. Real Actions. Family Influence.**

**Discover → Do → Report → Influence**

Use the wording **evidence-informed** or **research-backed design principles**. Do not claim Gaia’s Secret Agents itself is scientifically proven to improve environmental outcomes.

## Email 2 — 1 day later

**Timing:** 24 hours after signup

**Subject:** Has your Agent reported back yet?

**Preview text:** The field assignment is not finished until Headquarters receives the debrief.

Hi,

If your child has completed The Nature Signal, there is one more part of the mission.

Ask them to report back to Headquarters:

https://gaiassecretagents.com/report-back-to-headquarters.html?utm_source=brevo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=free_mission_sequence&utm_content=email_2

They will be asked to confirm four things:

- they discovered something they had not noticed before
- they took one small Earth action
- they told someone what they discovered
- they invited the household to try one small positive action too

Completing all four unlocks **Field Verified** status and an Impact Record.

The family part is deliberate. Environmental-education research increasingly suggests that children can carry environmental learning into family conversations and behaviour, so GSA is designed to let the child be the Agent—not just the recipient of an adult lesson.

*GSA is evidence-informed; Gaia’s Secret Agents itself has not been scientifically evaluated as an intervention.*

## Metrics to watch

Primary conversion metric: **free mission signup → Starter Kit purchase**.

Secondary metrics:
- free mission download clicks
- Report Back page visits
- Starter Kit button clicks after Field Verified
- Email 3 Starter Kit clicks
- Mission 001 waitlist joins

Do not optimise for email open rate alone. The useful signal is whether families complete the mission and move into a paid GSA product.
