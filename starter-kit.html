Before the Agent opens the file

What the grown-up needs to prepare.

Printing

A4 colour printer, ordinary white paper, plus heavier card for pieces such as the wheel and collector card. The master includes exact print settings.

Simple craft supplies

Scissors, glue or strong double-sided tape, one split pin or paper fastener, and a pencil or pen.

Adult setup

Cut and assemble the wheel, cards, box and envelope before delivery. An adult should handle card piercing and supervise scissors and outdoor activity.

No specialist environmental equipment is required. The preparation is mainly printing, cutting and assembling the classified file. Secret Meeting Cards are designed to bring the family into the experience, while the recruited child keeps the central Agent identity.

Frequently asked questions

Before you recruit your Agent.

What age is Gaia’s Secret Agents for?

GSA is designed primarily for children aged 6–11. How much adult involvement is useful varies with the child’s age and confidence.

Is this a physical product?

No. The Starter Kit is a digital product designed to be printed at home. Nothing needs to arrive in the post before your Agent can begin.

Is it screen-free?

The materials are delivered digitally, but the experience itself is designed to move children away from screens and into real-world investigation and activity.

Does my child need an email address?

No. The Agency communicates through the grown-up. A child email address is not required.

How many pages are in the Starter Kit?

The current Starter Kit is a 33-page master PDF. It includes the grown-up preparation guide, story, Agent identity pieces, first assignment, 28-card Secret Meeting deck and box, Bramblewick Collector Card, official credentials, Mission 001 teaser and the printable postal envelope.

What do I need at home?

You need an A4 colour printer, white paper, some heavier card or thin recycled cardboard, scissors, glue or strong double-sided tape, one split pin or paper fastener, and a pencil or pen.

Can the family join in?

Yes. The recruited child holds the central Agent identity, while the Secret Meeting deck is specifically designed for children and grown-ups to talk, imagine and join in together.

Is this about frightening children with environmental problems?

No. GSA focuses on curiosity, agency and achievable action rather than doom-and-gloom messaging.

What happens after the Starter Kit?

The Starter Kit is the beginning. As new missions become available, Agents can continue building their record, meeting characters and investigating the natural world as part of the Agency.
