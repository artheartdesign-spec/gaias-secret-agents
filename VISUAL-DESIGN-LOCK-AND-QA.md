## Approved visual decisions — preserve
- Official Gaia's Secret Agents round logo only; do not redraw, recolour or substitute.
- Palette: dark navy, antique gold, parchment and vivid teal; premium, bright, legible.
- Family-focused messaging. Teachers and schools retain dedicated resources.
- Minimum ordinary reading text: 16 pt (approximately 21.33 CSS px at standard CSS conversion). Decorative UI labels may use 16 CSS px when needed for fit; do not shrink normal reading copy.
- Maintain approved product artwork, prices, character canon and page copy unless a defect requires a targeted fix.
- Optical positioning: slightly more breathing room below than above; avoid cramped lower edges.
- No tilted/overlaid stamps obstructing text. Prefer simple unrotated labels integrated in normal layout.
- Mobile navigation and buttons must wrap visibly, not hide or overlap.
- No fabricated representations of actual printable pages. Mark illustrative mock-ups honestly.
