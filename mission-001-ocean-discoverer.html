Mission dossier

What is inside the planned pack?

Mission 001 is being designed as a complete printable adventure rather than a loose collection of worksheets. The goal is a child who finishes the pack feeling that they investigated something, learned something and changed something.

01 · Classified briefing

The Agency receives an ocean signal and gives the Agent a clear investigation objective.

02 · Ocean clue hunt

Search for patterns, clues and evidence that reveal how an ocean habitat works.

03 · Wildlife field log

Observe, sketch and record animal evidence without needing specialist equipment.

04 · Secret code

Crack an age-appropriate code to reveal the next stage of the investigation.

05 · Spot the problem

Identify an environmental problem and separate observations from assumptions.

06 · Design a marine sanctuary

Use evidence to design a safe habitat and explain why each feature matters.

07 · Earth Protector action

Choose one realistic action that can help protect the local environment.

08 · Agent completion record

Finish the case with a mission report, completion credential and collectible mission record.

Designed for ages 6–11. The final pack will include a grown-up preparation guide, printable pages and clear instructions so the child can experience the mission as an unfolding story.
