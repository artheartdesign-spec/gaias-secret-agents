Preparation checklist

  • A4 colour printer
  • white A4 paper
  • heavier card or thin recycled cardboard
  • 160–180 gsm paper for the postal envelope if available
  • scissors
  • glue or strong double-sided tape
  • one split pin / paper fastener
  • pencil or pen
Print carefully: the Starter Kit includes different print settings for story pages, wheels, duplex cards, the deck box and the postal envelope. Follow page 1 of the 33-page master before printing the full file.
