Digital art FAQ

Questions about printing the artwork

What do I receive with the Golden Wren digital art print?

The master artwork is prepared as a 6000 × 9000 pixel JPG with 300 DPI metadata and a 2:3 aspect ratio. It is a digital file; no physical item is mailed.

What sizes can I print from a 2:3 art file?

The same composition fits 4 × 6, 8 × 12, 12 × 18, 16 × 24 and 20 × 30 inch frames. Those sizes all use the same 2:3 proportion.

Can I print the artwork at home?

Yes, especially at smaller sizes. For larger wall prints, a local or online professional print service will usually give more consistent paper and colour results.

Is this a physical print?

No. The collection is digital wall art. You download the artwork and arrange printing yourself, so there is no shipping charge or delivery wait.
