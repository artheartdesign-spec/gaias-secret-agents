Free Mini Missions

Four Tiny Missions. One Big Adventure.

Need something children can start right now? Pick one mission, grab a pencil and head outside. These quick activities turn ordinary nature into a Secret Agent investigation.

Mission 01

The 5-Minute Nature Detective

Your mission: Find 3 signs that an animal has been nearby.

  • Feathers
  • Tracks
  • Chewed leaves
  • Spider webs
  • Nibbled seeds
  • Tiny holes
Agent Report: What did you find? Where was it? What animal might have left the clue?

Secret Agent Tip: Don't disturb the evidence. Observe, record and leave it where you found it.

Mission 02

The Sound Map

Your mission: Sit quietly outside for 3 minutes. Close your eyes and listen.

Draw a small circle to represent yourself. Add a mark for every sound you hear.

  • What made the sound?
  • Where was it?
  • Was it close or far away?
Agent Challenge: Can you find one sound you normally don't notice?
Mission 03

The Tiny World Hunt

Your mission: Find 5 tiny things in nature.

  • A tiny leaf
  • A seed
  • A flower
  • An insect
  • A piece of bark
  • Moss or a feather

Don't pick anything living.

Agent Report: Choose your favourite discovery and sketch it. Describe it without using its name. Can another agent guess what it is?
Mission 04

The Earth Protector Test

Your mission: Find one small way to help your local environment today.

  • Pick up a safe piece of litter.
  • Look after a plant.
  • Keep a natural area undisturbed.
  • Remind someone to save water.
  • Spend 10 minutes observing nature instead of using a screen.
Agent Promise: “I can make a difference through small actions.” Write your own Earth Protector promise.
